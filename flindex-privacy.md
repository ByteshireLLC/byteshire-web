Flindex is made by Byteshire LLC ("we," "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Flindex app on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

## The short version

**Flindex does not collect your personal data.** We have no servers, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Flindex reads the files in folders you choose so it can build a fast, private search index of what's inside them — and that index stays on your device and, through Apple's iCloud, in your own private iCloud account, which only you can access. We never see your files or your index.

## What Flindex reads, and why

You point Flindex at one or more folders you already keep — in iCloud Drive, on your Mac, or in another cloud. With your permission, Flindex reads the files in those folders to extract their text and build a search index, so you can later find a file by a word or phrase from inside it. Flindex only accesses the folders you explicitly grant it, and it reads your files solely to index them and to show you a file when you open it.

Flindex does **not** move, copy off your device, or take ownership of your files. They stay exactly where they are — in the cloud or on the disk you already use — which continues to own their storage, syncing, and sharing.

## What Flindex stores, and where

Flindex builds a **search index** and keeps it on your device, mirroring it to your **private iCloud database** through Apple's CloudKit so your search works across your signed-in devices. This data lives in your personal Apple account; Byteshire cannot read it. The index contains:

- **Extracted text** — the searchable text pulled from your files. This can include the contents of your documents, which is what makes searching inside them possible.
- **File metadata** — each file's name, folder path, size, modification date, a content fingerprint, and file type.

If you turn off iCloud for Flindex, the index stays only on your device. The index is derived entirely from your own files and can always be rebuilt, so removing it never affects the files themselves.

## Opening and editing your files

When you open a search result, Flindex hands the file to the system to preview it, open it in its default app, or reveal it in the Finder on a Mac — nothing is sent to us. If you edit a text or Markdown file inside Flindex, your changes are written back to that file in place, in the folder where it already lives. Your edits never pass through Byteshire.

## No analytics or tracking

Flindex contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software. It makes no network requests to Byteshire, and full-text search runs entirely on your device, offline. The only network activity is Apple's own iCloud syncing of your private index between your devices, which Apple handles within your account.

## Children

Flindex is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your control and data deletion

Because your index lives on your device and in your own iCloud, you control it. To remove it, delete the app and, if you wish, remove Flindex's data from iCloud in your device's iCloud settings. Your source files are untouched by this — they remain wherever you keep them.
