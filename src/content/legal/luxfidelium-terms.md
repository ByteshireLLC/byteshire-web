## Lux Fidelium Premium subscription

Praying, browsing, and syncing in Lux Fidelium are free. **Lux Fidelium Premium** is an optional auto-renewing subscription that supports the app and adds features on top of it.

- **What Premium unlocks:** your full prayer statistics, including the minutes you've prayed and the prayers you return to most, and a choice of color themes, including themes that follow the liturgical year. Your count of completed prayers is always free. More features may be added to Premium over time.
- **Subscription options:**
  - **Monthly** — $0.99 (US) per month.
  - **Yearly** — $9.99 (US) per year.
- **Free trial:** new subscribers get a free trial of **two weeks** on the monthly plan or **one month** on the yearly plan. If you have previously used a trial, you may not be eligible again.
- **Family Sharing:** a subscription can be shared with up to five other members of your Apple Family Sharing group.
- Prices are in US dollars and may vary by region; the exact price, currency, and any applicable taxes are shown in the app before you confirm a purchase.
- Your statistics are kept whether or not you subscribe, so if your subscription ends, nothing is lost and the full statistics return if you subscribe again.

- Payment is charged to your Apple ID account when you confirm the purchase.
- Your subscription **automatically renews** at the then-current price unless you cancel it at least **24 hours before** the end of the current period.
