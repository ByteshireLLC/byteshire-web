Cirth is made by Byteshire LLC ("we," "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Cirth app on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

## The short version

**Cirth does not collect your personal data, and we never see your documents.** We have no servers, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Cirth opens the files you choose and saves your changes back to those same files. Two small things can sync through **your own iCloud account**, which we cannot read: the names of the tags you use, and, if you have bought Cirth Unlock, where you stopped reading each document. Your documents themselves never pass through Cirth's sync.

## What Cirth reads, and why

Cirth reads a file when you open it — by picking it in the file browser, double-clicking it, or dragging it in. It reads that file to display and edit it, and nothing else. Cirth cannot reach into folders you have not opened: it runs in Apple's App Sandbox with access only to the files and folders you explicitly hand it.

If a document is changed by another app or in a terminal while you have it open, Cirth notices and reloads it, so the version you see is the one on disk.

## What Cirth stores, and where

Cirth does **not** keep its own copy of your documents. Your edits are written back to the original file, in place, wherever it already lives — on your device, in iCloud Drive, or in another cloud provider's folder. That provider continues to own the file's storage and syncing exactly as before.

What Cirth stores on your device is small:

- **Your preferences,** such as line spacing, theme, and where the document outline appears.
- **The system's record of which documents you had open,** so they can reopen. On a Mac that includes the security-scoped bookmarks macOS uses to restore access to a file you opened before.
- **Where you stopped reading** each document, its reading mode and its folded headings. This is shared on your device with other Byteshire apps you install, such as Flindex, so a document opens at the same place in each.
- **The names of the tags** your documents use, so every Byteshire app on the device offers the same tags.
- **Your highlights and notes** on a Cirth document, which are saved inside that document's own bundle, next to its text.
- **Performance records:** if Cirth pauses or crashes, it keeps a short record on your device of what it was doing, which you can read in Settings. It is never sent anywhere by Cirth.

None of these contain your documents' text.

## What syncs through your iCloud

Cirth uses Apple's iCloud, in your own private iCloud database, for two things. We have no access to that database, and Apple's privacy policy governs it.

- **Tag names.** The names of the tags in your documents, such as "travel" or "recipes," sync so that every Byteshire app on all your devices offers the same tags, and a tag you rename in one app is renamed in the others. This happens for everyone who is signed in to iCloud.
- **Reading places, with Cirth Unlock.** If you have bought Cirth Unlock, where you stopped reading each document in iCloud Drive syncs to your other devices: an identifier for the file, your position in it, its reading mode and its folded headings. Places for files outside iCloud Drive stay on the device.

The text of your documents, their pictures, and their highlights and notes never travel through this sync.

## Network requests Cirth makes for you

Cirth contacts the internet only when a document or an action of yours calls for it, and never contacts Byteshire:

- **Remote images.** If a document refers to an image by its web address, Cirth loads it from the server that hosts it, so it can be shown. If you add a picture from the web to a Cirth document, Cirth downloads it from that server and saves it inside the document.
- **YouTube videos.** If a document links to a YouTube video, Cirth shows its poster image, and loads YouTube's player only when you press play. The player comes from youtube-nocookie.com, run by Google, under Google's privacy policy.
- **iCloud,** for the sync described above.
- **Purchases.** Cirth Unlock is sold through Apple's App Store, which handles payment. We receive no payment information and do not learn who you are.

Those servers see the request, as any website you visit would. None of it passes through Byteshire.

## Read Aloud

Read Aloud uses your device's own speech voices. Your document's text is spoken on the device and is not sent anywhere.

## No analytics or tracking

Cirth contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software, and it builds no profile of you or your usage. If you have opted in to sharing diagnostics with Apple at the system level, Apple may send us anonymized crash and performance reports through App Store Connect; those come from Apple, are not linked to your identity, and contain no document content.

## Children

Cirth is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your control and data deletion

There is nothing of yours for us to delete, because we never receive anything. Deleting the app removes its preferences and the records it keeps on your device. Your files are untouched — they remain wherever you keep them, in the same format any other Markdown or text editor can read.

To remove what Cirth synced through iCloud, open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, or System Settings on your Mac, then your name, then iCloud, and manage your account's storage. Turning off iCloud for Cirth stops the sync.
