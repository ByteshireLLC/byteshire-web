Gravitas is made by Byteshire LLC ("we," "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Gravitas app on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

## The short version

**Gravitas does not collect your personal data.** We have no servers, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Gravitas draws black holes by computing how light travels around them, entirely on your device. It makes no network requests at all, and it reads none of your files, photos, contacts, or location.

## What Gravitas stores, and where

While you use Gravitas, it keeps a small performance log on your device: when the app paused or hung, how much memory it used, and diagnostic reports that Apple's MetricKit hands to the app on your device. Each entry notes which part of the app you were using, such as moving a slider or opening a preset, so a slowdown can be traced to its cause. The log holds nothing about you, and it never leaves your device on its own. You can read it, export it, or clear it from the app's The Science screen; if you export it, the file goes only where you choose to send it.

## No analytics or tracking

Gravitas contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software, and builds no profile of you or your usage. If you have opted in to sharing diagnostics with app developers at the system level, Apple may send us anonymized crash and performance reports through App Store Connect; those come from Apple, are not linked to your identity, and contain nothing about you.

## Children

Gravitas is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your control and data deletion

There is nothing of yours for us to delete, because we never receive anything. Clearing the performance log, or deleting the app, removes everything Gravitas kept on your device.
